## Issues detected while filling out the CST
The CST comprises of all signals controlled by the control unit. It indicates which control signals activate during which machine cycles
* Explored the possibility of making the MAX select multiplexer signal based on the opCode
	- After looking at the relevant opCodes, the MAX_sel signal could be wired directly to IW[6], reducing necessary complexity in the control unit
* Concluded pipelining is not really necessary for the current project
	- The current architecture utilizes bits within the instruction word itself to control various multiplexers within the datapath. This makes pipelining the current design virtually impossible, and would require significant fundamental redesign of the datapath, increasing the complexity and risk of data hazards significantly
