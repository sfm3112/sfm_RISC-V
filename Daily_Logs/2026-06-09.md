## BLTU and BGEU
* One idea to do unsigned comparisons would be to look at the carry bit instead of the negative bit
* This would work if the 32-bit number is actually 33-bit, where the MSB of each number is always zero, thus maintaining the unsigned component
* Then the old carry bit is now a sign bit, correctly denoting positive and negative numbers
* Branch less than unsigned will trigger when the carry bit is zero, and Branch greater than or equal to unsigned will trigger if the carry bit is 1
