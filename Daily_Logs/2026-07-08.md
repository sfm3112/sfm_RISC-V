## Root of srai Bug
* The issue derives from the immediate itself
* During the design process, the immediate for all immediate types was assumed to be bits 31:20
* For shifts only 5 of the immediate bits are necessary to complete a full shift
* of the remaining 7 bits, one is used to differentiate between a shift left and shift right
* While this bit was accounted for in the ALU, it was not accounted for in the immediate processor leading an immediate of 0x0001 to appear 0x0401
* The first solution being attempted is to pass the lower two bits of Funct3 and Opcode[4] to the immediate processor and if it matches those of the shift immediates, only extend 5 bits (shamt) instead of all 12
* This should check {IW[13:12], IW[4]} and if it is 011 then pass the immediate associated with shift immediates

## First Debug Attempt
Attempted to check the Instruction word for if its a shift immediate and alter the immediate accordingly
* Determined that this is not going to work, as its not the source of the problem, the ALU should cut the immdediate down to 5 bits anyway

## Second Debug Attempt
Looking into the ALU itself, it is determined that the issue is that the signal differentiating shift right logic and arithmetic is incorrect and always sets to shift right logic
